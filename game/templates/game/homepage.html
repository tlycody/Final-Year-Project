How to Play

Phishing Ninja

An email will slide onto the screen from the bottom. Highlight the parts that look suspicious, then choose whether the email is phishing or safe.

Deepfake Detective

A scenario card appears — voice call, video, post or article. Spot AI-generated content by highlighting suspicious phrases and identifying the threat type.

URL Hunter

URLs float around the screen. Click any that look suspicious, then choose the part that is unsafe (protocol, domain, TLD, or path). Remove them before time runs out.
