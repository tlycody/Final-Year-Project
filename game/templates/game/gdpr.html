GDPR & Data Privacy Information

Data Collection and Usage

This cybersecurity training platform is developed as part of a research project at the University of Exeter. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in accordance with GDPR regulations.

What Data We Collect

  • Account information (username, email)
  • Game performance and progress data
  • Survey responses for research purposes
  • Login timestamps and session information

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Providing the training game functionality
  • Academic research on cybersecurity education effectiveness
  • Improving the platform and learning experience
  • Anonymized statistical analysis

Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct any inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Opt out of data collection at any time

Data Security

All data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and security practices. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized research personnel only.

Data Retention

Personal data will be retained for the duration of the research project and up to 5 years afterward for academic purposes. Anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for research purposes.
